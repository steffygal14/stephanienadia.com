about

hi, I'm stephanie.

new york city

I'm a Brooklyn-born creative and storyteller with Trinidadian and Ecuadorian roots, proudly married into Haitian culture, raising two boys in New York City.

I built my first decade in fashion and beauty, partnering with brands including Bulgari, Steve Madden and Drunk Elephant, making national commercial appearances, and developing a full production practice behind the camera as well as in front of it.

Then I stepped away for three years to raise my family, and came back to what we do every day: eat, cook, and explore this city together. Honest restaurant reviews, recipes worth saving, family outings, and the life in between.

Food is where we start. Life is what we share. We would love for you to pull up a chair, dine with us, sit with us, laugh with us, and make the most of life and the people you love, wherever you are.
