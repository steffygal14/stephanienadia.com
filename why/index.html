why work with us

we make content the way we live.

With care, with taste, and with people at the table.

1

new york, born and raised.

Brooklyn-born, with Trinidadian and Ecuadorian roots, proudly married into Haitian culture, and raising two boys in the city we grew up in. We know where to eat, what is worth the line, and who is really cooking.

2

artists and storytellers first.

More than a decade in photography, videography and design, in front of the camera and behind it. Everything is shot, edited and styled in-house, so the work looks like a campaign and feels like a friend's recommendation.

3

a real table, a real family.

Our audience watches a family of four eat, explore and make a home in New York. Recommendations land because we know good food, and as native New Yorkers we are discerning about where it is worth going.

4

a table with room for everyone.

Our cooking starts in Trinidadian, Ecuadorian and Haitian kitchens and moves freely through Asian and American food too. It is a cross-cultural table you don't often see in everyday recipes and households.

5

honest by habit.

We only feature what we would serve at our own table, and we say so on camera. All partnerships are disclosed per FTC guidelines.

6

everything under one roof.

Content, production and social media management, through Stephanie Nadia and Aedi Agency. One conversation, one team, one standard.
